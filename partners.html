FOOD SOVEREIGNTY · SLOW FOOD

Slow Food

The camp's Navajo-Churro lamb and mutton is safeguarded as a Slow Food Presidium — a heritage food championed across the Slow Food network, from the global movement to Arizona's local chapters and the Indigenous-led Turtle Island Association.
